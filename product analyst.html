New user activation for an AI audio storytelling app

Funnel analysis and experiment design on live product data, PostHog and Python, 2026

Question
Where do new users drop off between installing the app and their first listen?
What I found
Only 5.7% of installers reached a first listen. Most of the loss happened before onboarding even began, and the genres screen was the biggest leak inside onboarding: 24% of users who reached it quit, and it took four times longer than the screen before it.
What I recommended
An A/B test making genre selection optional, which I designed and configured in PostHog, with retention and recommendation clicks as guardrails and a 5-week run sized to detect a 5-point lift.

Before trusting the numbers, I caught and fixed several tracking issues: two competing install events, a "first listen" event that fired automatically, and a conditional screen that would have looked like a drop-off.

Where should a mobile game place its first gate?

A/B test analysis of 90,189 players, Python, 2026

Question
Does moving Cookie Cats' first gate from level 30 to level 40 change player retention?
What I found
The later gate lowered 7-day retention, a statistically significant drop confirmed by a bootstrap analysis.
Level 30
19.0%
Level 40
18.2%
What I recommended
Keep the gate at level 30, verify the assignment logic after a sample ratio check flagged a small imbalance, and test dynamic gates for fast-progressing players next.
